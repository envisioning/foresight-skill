# Scan types

Envisioning Signals has seven playbooks, one per kind of research question. Use them to set the scope and the source mix of a scan. Source: https://signals.envisioning.com/llms.txt.

| Scan | Question it answers | Scope | Main sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Horizon scan | What signals of change across the macro environment could shape long-horizon strategy? | Unbounded | Open literature, policy documents, scientific publications, reputable press |
| Technology scan | What is real in this technology space, who builds it, and how mature is it? | One industry, sector, technical field or region | Open literature, product landscape, standards bodies, regulation |
| Regulatory scan | What rulemaking, enforcement and standards activity is coming, in force, or being interpreted? | One jurisdiction and topic | Regulator publications, statutes, court rulings, standards bodies, legal press |
| Risk radar | What downside indicators are appearing across the organization's exposure? | Operational, geopolitical, supply chain, cyber, physical climate and reputational risk | Incident reports, regulator and agency advisories, scientific assessments, reputable press |
| Competitive scan | Where are named competitors placing bets, and how is their posture shifting? | A named set of competitors in one category | Product pages, filings, job postings, public messaging, trade press |
| Innovation radar | What new practices, business models and operating patterns change how work gets done? | Practices and value creation, not raw technology | Case studies, company disclosures, conference talks, scholarly work, business press |
| Adjacency scan | Where to play next: adjacent categories, customers, regions, channels and capabilities? | Relative to a defined home position | Industry filings, market data, product landscapes, trade press |

For a technology scan, separate what is deployed, what is piloted and what is only announced, with evidence for each call.

Envisioning's open research hubs (`list_hubs`) are finished technology scans of whole domains. Start there when a hub matches the scope, and add signals the hub does not cover.
